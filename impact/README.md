# 🎓 Impact — Academic Decision Impact Platform

> **Prospective Decision Intelligence & Compliance Infrastructure for Higher Education**

Please refer to the master [README.md](../../README.md) at the repository root for the complete system architecture, operational flow, and technical breakdown.

Impact evaluates the downstream consequences of academic decisions (dropping courses, term withdrawals, changing majors, reducing credits) before they occur, giving students and advisors the deterministic foresight needed to protect financial aid, scholarship eligibility, and graduation timelines.
